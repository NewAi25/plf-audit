# Duplication check

Started 16 September 2026. The question for every source is the same: does it already specify what a PLF welfare system must log and expose so that a third party can verify its claim, or score products against such a specification?

## ICAR

Not yet checked against Section 11. What the saved page `corpus/icar_validated_sensors.html` shows on 16 September 2026, recorded here as fact, not yet as analysis. Three systems are listed as validated: Ekomilk Horizon Unlimited Instrument, a milk analyser measuring somatic cell count and milk parameters; Brolis BLH01, an inline sensor measuring milk fat and protein; and the Panazoo MMI system, measuring milk yield. All three are dairy cattle, and all three measure milk composition or yield. None measures a welfare indicator. The page states that ICAR validation confirms a device meets manufacturer performance claims through an ICAR approved test plan, for the purpose the applicant defines, and that success leads to an ICAR Statement of Validation.

The application checklist on the page asks for a description of all system components, the system technical manual, the farm operator manual, internal research and validation studies, peer reviewed publications, the software manual, the installation procedure, routine or periodic checking procedures for service technicians, and technical drawings. That checklist is the starting point for the disclosure dimension in column 4.
