# PLF Welfare Auditability Standard, dairy v0.1

Draft skeleton. Frozen in week 4.

## Purpose

What the standard is for and who uses it: a certifier accepting sensor evidence first, ICAR second.

## Scope

A summary of `scope_note.md`, which sets out what is in and out of scope and the position the standard does not take.

## Definitions

Definitions of welfare claim, indicator, validated indicator, external validation, raw reading, alert and lineage.

## Structure

The four columns: validated indicators, what the sensor measures, what certification requires, and what the system must log and prove.

How an assessor scores a product against `rubric.md` from public documentation, including how NA is recorded.

How the standard and the scorecard are versioned, and how next year's assessment is run as a comparison with this one.

## What this standard does not cover

The limits: one species, public documentation only, no field validation of the standard, and no independent assessment of the welfare science.
